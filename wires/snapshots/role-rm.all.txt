Drop a role no service names any more, and publish

Usage: wires role rm [OPTIONS] <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>  The role to drop

Options:
      --state-ttl <TTL>  Lifetime of the new policy, from now; never shortens the current one [default: 90d]
  -h, --help             Print help
      --help-all         Also list the operator and credential flags

Example:
  wires role rm analyst
