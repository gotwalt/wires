Admit a node: mint its badge and print its join token (edits no policy)

Usage: wires invite [OPTIONS] <NODE_ID>

Arguments:
  <NODE_ID>  The joiner's node id (what `wires id` printed on its machine)

Options:
      --name <NAME>  A local label for it, for `wires remove` and `wires service add --host`
      --ttl <TTL>    Lifetime of the invitee's badge (`30d`, `12h`, … or seconds; at most 30d) [default: 30d]
  -h, --help         Print help
      --help-all     Also list the operator and credential flags

Example:
  wires invite <node id> --name alice    # prints the token; send it to them
