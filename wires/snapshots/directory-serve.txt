Run this node's directory alone (no host.json) until Ctrl-C

Usage: wires directory serve [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --max-subscribers <MAX_SUBSCRIBERS>
          How many hosts may follow the policy here at once (one more is refused) [default: 4096]
  -h, --help
          Print help
      --help-all
          Also list the operator and credential flags

Example:
  wires directory serve
