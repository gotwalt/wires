Drop a service and publish the new policy

Usage: wires service rm [OPTIONS] <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>  The service to drop

Options:
      --state-ttl <TTL>  Lifetime of the new policy, from now; never shortens the current one [default: 90d]
  -h, --help             Print help
      --help-all         Also list the operator and credential flags

Example:
  wires service rm orders-db
