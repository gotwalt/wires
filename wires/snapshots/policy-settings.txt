Print the network's settings, or change them and publish

Usage: wires policy settings [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --freshness <FRESHNESS>
          When no directory has vouched for a host's policy lately: keep deciding, or refuse

          Possible values:
          - lenient: Keep deciding under the held policy until it expires
          - strict:  Refuse calls until a directory vouches again

      --beat-secs <BEAT_SECS>
          How often each directory signs a new freshness timestamp, in seconds

      --fresh-secs <FRESH_SECS>
          How long each freshness timestamp is good for, in seconds (at least the beat)

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

      --help-all
          Also list the operator and credential flags

Examples:
  wires policy settings
  wires policy settings --freshness strict --beat-secs 60
