Print the network's settings, or change them and publish

Usage: wires policy settings [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --beat-secs <BEAT_SECS>    How often each directory signs a new freshness timestamp, in seconds
      --fresh-secs <FRESH_SECS>  How long each freshness timestamp is good for, in seconds (at least the beat)
  -h, --help                     Print help
      --help-all                 Also list the operator and credential flags

Examples:
  wires policy settings
  wires policy settings --fresh-secs 300 --beat-secs 60
