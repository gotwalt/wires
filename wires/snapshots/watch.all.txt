Stream the call records you may read, verified, from the services' hosts

Usage: wires watch [OPTIONS] [SERVICE]...

Arguments:
  [SERVICE]...  The services to watch (default: every service you may call or read)

Options:
      --mine                   Only your own calls, even where you may read everyone's
      --json                   One JSON object per record: `{service, host, seq, entry}`
      --once                   Print what is there (after your last mark) and exit; don't follow
      --relay-url <RELAY_URL>  Dial through this relay instead of the n0 default
  -h, --help                   Print help
      --help-all               Also list the operator and credential flags

Examples:
  wires watch orders-db
  wires watch --mine --once

Readers the policy names see a service's calls in full; everyone else sees
their own. Exit 77: every host refused the stream.
