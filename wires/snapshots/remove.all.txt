Ban a node until its badge expires; hosts refuse its next call

Usage: wires remove [OPTIONS] <MEMBER>

Arguments:
  <MEMBER>  The node to remove: its `wires invite --name` label, or its node id

Options:
      --state-ttl <STATE_TTL>  Lifetime of the new signed policy, from now (`90d`, `12h`, … or seconds). Never shortens the current policy's expiry [default: 90d]
  -h, --help                   Print help
      --help-all               Also list the operator and credential flags

Example:
  wires remove alice
