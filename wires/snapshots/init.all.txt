Create the network: the root key, this node's key and badge, the first policy

Usage: wires init [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --ttl <TTL>
          Lifetime of this node's badge (`30d`, `12h`, … or seconds; at most 30d) [default: 30d]
      --state-ttl <STATE_TTL>
          Lifetime of the first signed policy [default: 90d]
      --issuer <ISSUER>
          The IdP the network trusts first: its exact `iss` (more: `wires issuer set`) [default: https://accounts.google.com]
      --client-id <CLIENT_ID>
          The OAuth client id `wires login` signs in under (or `$WIRES_OIDC_CLIENT_ID`)
      --audience <AUDIENCE>
          An `aud` value hosts accept from that IdP (repeatable; default: the client id)
      --public-client-secret <PUBLIC_CLIENT_SECRET>
          That client's public secret, which invites carry to `wires login`
  -h, --help
          Print help
      --help-all
          Also list the operator and credential flags

Example:
  wires init --client-id <desktop client id> --public-client-secret <its secret>
