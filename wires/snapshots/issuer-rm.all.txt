Stop trusting an IdP no role names any more, and publish

Usage: wires issuer rm [OPTIONS] <ISSUER>

Arguments:
  <ISSUER>  The IdP's exact `iss`

Options:
      --state-ttl <TTL>  Lifetime of the new policy, from now; never shortens the current one [default: 90d]
  -h, --help             Print help
      --help-all         Also list the operator and credential flags

Example:
  wires issuer rm https://idp.example.com
