Send a caller a message by node id or role, to its inbox (logged)

Usage: wires push [OPTIONS] --to <TO> --subject <SUBJECT> [BODY]...

Arguments:
  [BODY]...  The body; read from stdin when none is given (and stdin isn't a terminal)

Options:
      --to <TO>            A node id (a service's `$WIRES_CALLER_NODE`), or a role in the policy
      --subject <SUBJECT>  One line, shown in the recipient's inbox
      --ttl <TTL>          How long to keep it for a recipient not listening (`90m`, `2d`; max 7d)
  -h, --help               Print help

Example:
  wires push --to "$WIRES_CALLER_NODE" --subject "build 42 done" "all green"
